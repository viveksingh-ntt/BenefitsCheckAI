# Overview
Harbor HMO covers care from doctors, hospitals and labs in the Harbor HMO network. You choose a primary care doctor (PCP) who coordinates your care. Out-of-network care is not covered except for emergencies and urgent care when you are away from the service area.

# Deductible and out-of-pocket limit
The individual deductible is $1,500 per calendar year and the family deductible is $3,000. Copays for office visits do not count toward the deductible and are not subject to it. The individual out-of-pocket limit is $6,000 and the family limit is $12,000. Copays, coinsurance and the deductible count toward the out-of-pocket limit.

# Office visits
Primary care visit: $20 copay. Specialist visit: $40 copay. A referral from your PCP is required before you see a specialist, including a dermatologist. Telehealth visits with network doctors: $10 copay.

# Dermatology
Medically necessary dermatology office visits, such as an exam of a changing mole or a specific skin concern, are covered at the specialist copay of $40 per visit with a PCP referral. Skin biopsies and lab work are covered at 20% coinsurance after the deductible. Mohs surgery requires prior authorization. Cosmetic services, such as treatment of wrinkles or cosmetic skin evaluations, are excluded.

# Preventive care
Preventive care from network providers, including annual check-ups and recommended screenings, is covered at no cost. A full-body skin check is not a preventive service under this plan unless your doctor finds it medically necessary.

# Emergency and urgent care
Emergency room: $250 copay, waived if admitted. Urgent care center: $50 copay. Emergency care is covered in and out of network.
