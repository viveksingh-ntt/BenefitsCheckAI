# Overview
Harbor PPO lets you see any doctor. You pay less with Harbor PPO network providers. You do not need to choose a primary care doctor and you do not need referrals to see specialists.

# Deductible and out-of-pocket limit
The individual deductible is $1,000 in network and $2,000 out of network per calendar year. Copays for network office visits are not subject to the deductible. The individual out-of-pocket limit is $5,000 in network and $10,000 out of network.

# Office visits
Primary care visit: $25 copay in network. Specialist visit: $60 copay in network. Out-of-network office visits: 40% coinsurance after the out-of-network deductible.

# Dermatology
Medically necessary dermatology visits are covered at the $60 specialist copay in network, or 40% coinsurance after the deductible out of network. No referral is needed. Skin biopsies are covered at 15% coinsurance after the deductible in network. Mohs surgery requires prior authorization. Cosmetic services are excluded.

# Emergency and urgent care
Emergency room: $300 copay, waived if admitted. Urgent care: $60 copay in network.
