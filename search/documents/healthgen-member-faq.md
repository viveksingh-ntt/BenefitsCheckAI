# What is a copay?
A copay is a fixed amount you pay for a covered service, such as $40 for a specialist visit. You usually pay it at the visit.

# What is a deductible?
The deductible is the amount you pay for covered services each year before the plan starts to share the cost through coinsurance. Some services, such as office visit copays, are not subject to the deductible.

# What is coinsurance?
Coinsurance is your share of the cost of a covered service as a percentage, such as 20%, usually after you meet your deductible.

# How do I find an in-network doctor?
Use Find Care in the member portal or ask the care assistant. Network status can change, so confirm with the office when you book.

# How do I cancel an appointment?
Open Appointments in the member portal and select Cancel, or call the doctor's office at least 24 hours ahead.

# How do I talk to a person?
Ask the care assistant to connect you with a care specialist, or call Member Services at the number on your ID card. Care specialists are available Monday to Friday, 8 am to 8 pm.
